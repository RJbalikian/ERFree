## Information
The ERFree web app is developed at the Illinois State Geological Survey (ISGS), part of the Prairie Research Institute at the University of Illinois.
Its purpose is to provide simple, web-based processing of 2D Electrical Resistivity Tomography (ER, or ERT) data for users of many skill levels.
This app has been tested on data from the ABEM Terrameter instrument, but may work with data from other instruments as well.

The ERFree app makes use of [pyGIMLi](https://www.pygimli.org/) for all ERT mesh and inversion processing algorithms. It is essentially a graphical user interface to the pyGIMLi ERT processing workflow.
pyGIMLi was chosen for its high degree of accuracy, relatively intuitive interface, quality of documentation, and review of literature. 
For example, ***Doyoro et al., 2022*** *: A review of open software resources in python for electrical resistivity modelling.* [https://doi.org/10.1186/s40562-022-00214-1](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40562-022-00214-1) was an important consideration in using pyGIMLi, as well as ERFree's creator's own experience.
